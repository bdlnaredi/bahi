Bahi Sync
=========

Bahi Sync copies your TallyPrime books to your Bahi server, so you can see them in the Bahi app on your
phone or computer. It only reads from Tally; the only thing it ever puts into Tally is a new entry you made
in the Bahi app (receipts, payments, orders, sales, purchases). It never changes or deletes anything in Tally.

Getting started
  1. Open Bahi Sync (Start menu, Bahi Sync).
  2. Settings: enter your Bahi server address and the sync token, then Test connection and Save.
  3. Keep TallyPrime open with your companies loaded. Bahi Sync checks Tally every 2 minutes.

More than one company
  Click Companies. Tick every company you want in the Bahi app and, if you like, a start date for each.
  Tally only lets Bahi read the companies that are open in it, so open all of them together
  (Alt+F3, Select Company) or have TallyPrime open them when it starts (F1, Settings, Startup).
  In the app, tap the company name at the top to switch between companies.

Files in this folder
  BahiSync.exe               the app
  TallySync.ps1 and others   the sync itself (kept up to date from your Bahi server)
  TallySync.config.json      your settings
  TallySync.sent             entries already put into Tally (stops one going in twice) - keep it
  TallySync.log              what happened at each sync

Removing it
  Windows Settings, Apps, Bahi Sync, Uninstall. Your data on the Bahi server is not touched.
